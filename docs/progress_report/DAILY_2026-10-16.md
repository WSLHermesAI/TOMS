## Watch out

- **Talking** works as before: the player steps onto the NPC's tile, or presses Enter while standing on it.
- **An NPC on several floors** shares its topics on all of them unless a topic sets *Talked to on*.
- **Chat lines and after-dialogues repeat**, so the test fails on rewards in them (`addStat`, `setStat`, `give`).
- **Android's fallback font** is coded and builds, but has not been seen running on a device.
- **Git LFS:** GitHub's free quota is 1 GB of storage and 1 GB of downloads a month; each clone downloads about
  100 MB of `Editor/bin`.
- **The event editor without the project folder** is a sandbox: Save marks the edits as saved but writes nothing.
