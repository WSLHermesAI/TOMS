How to play

  • Read the icon over the monster: the ring fills to the moment it hits. It glows red just before a heavy blow.
  • GUARD (F) just before a hit: Parry! (a heavy blow staggers it), Blocked, or Too early.
  • Attack by playing: when the attack is ready, your weapon's play waits over the monster — just play it (draw, swipe, tap). The ATTACK panel only shows ready / recovering. Bat jabs cut a play short — guard them.
  • Fill the break gauge (yellow under its HP): BREAK! stuns it, every hit ×1.5.
  • Greats and perfects fill the super (◉): ULTIMATE (U) — ×2, ×4 in a break.
  • Perfects in a row: Chain. A miss resets it.
  • Skills: 1 shield (blocks the next hit), 2 slow time.
Weaknesses: the golem is weak to the staff, the wraith to the bow, the bat to the sword.
