THE GODBORN
A Nobody’s Destiny

A funny, adventurous fantasy RPG with a heartfelt core: an ordinary orphan discovers a godlike power and sets out to rescue the parents who raised him—only to uncover a secret that could decide the fate of the world.

01 · Brainstorming

Five funny RPG premises

Each idea starts with a simple comedic hook that can grow into a larger adventure.

IDEA 01

The World’s Worst Hero

The prophecy chooses a hero whose only talent is running away. Every escape makes him stronger—so the party has to convince him to stop fleeing long enough to save the world.

Comedy hook: 100% escape success, 0% courage.

IDEA 02

I Accidentally Became the Demon King

An ordinary office worker is transported to a fantasy world and mistaken for the new Demon King. Monsters adore him; human kingdoms want him dead.

Comedy hook: He defeats enemies with awkward performance reviews.

IDEA 03

My Dad Is the Final Boss

A young adventurer discovers that the Demon King is his missing father, who may be protecting the world from something even worse.

Comedy hook: Boss fights are interrupted by embarrassing childhood stories.

IDEA 04

The Chicken That Saved the World

A farmer’s chicken eats a mysterious crystal and becomes the most powerful creature alive. It will only help if properly fed.

Comedy hook: Summon an army of furious chickens.

IDEA 05

The Last Save Point

A hero can save and reload reality, but each reload changes the world. NPCs begin remembering events that never happened—and the final boss notices the loop.

Comedy hook: The world gets increasingly annoyed by the player’s repeated attempts.

02 · Main story pitches

Five bigger storylines

These concepts are designed around a memorable opening, a strong personal motivation, and a twist that can reframe the adventure.

A. Save the Last Princess, Save the World — Adventure + Comedy

Opening: Demons attack the kingdom and kidnap the princess, who supposedly maintains the world’s magical balance. A broke adventurer volunteers only because he hears the rescue reward includes free dinner.

Twist: The princess is not helpless. She is the strongest person in the kingdom, but her power is sealed. The Demon King needs her help to stop an ancient evil, and the hero is the only one who can unlock her power.

Character dynamic: The princess repeatedly saves the hero while he insists he is the legendary chosen one. She knows he is making it up.

Ending direction: The pair discover that the true enemy is the ancient god who created the kingdom.

B. The Orphan Who Became a God — Coming of Age + Revenge

Opening: A poor orphan lives happily with his adoptive farmer parents. One night, mysterious soldiers burn the village, kidnap his parents, and leave him for dead. Before he loses consciousness, he hears: “Find the child. The prophecy says he is the last key.”

Awakening: He wakes surrounded by defeated monsters. He can see magical energy, understand ancient languages, and absorb abilities from creatures he defeats.

Twist: His parents were not ordinary farmers. They knew he was the last descendant of an ancient race called the Godborn and hid his identity so he could have a normal childhood.

Journey: Track the attackers, learn the truth about his powers, find his parents, uncover an empire collecting gifted children, and discover why the Demon King is trying to prevent an even greater catastrophe.

Emotional payoff: “We never wanted you to become a god. We just wanted you to have a happy childhood.”

C. The Hero Who Wasn’t Supposed to Exist — Fantasy + Mystery

Opening: Every thousand years, a Goddess chooses a hero to defeat the Demon King. This time, she chooses someone who has already died. He wakes with no memories, while strangers seem to know who he really is.

Twist: He is the previous Demon King, reincarnated without his memories. He once sacrificed himself to seal a greater evil. His former generals recognize him and keep trying to recruit him back.

Core choice: Reclaim his old power or destroy it forever.

D. My Little Sister Is the Final Boss — Family + Comedy

Opening: The kingdom declares that a young adventurer’s little sister is the reincarnation of the Dark Goddess and tries to execute her. He escapes with her to prove her innocence.

Twist: She really can destroy the world, but cannot control her power. When she gets angry, monsters appear; when she cries, the weather changes.

Gameplay hook: Protect her, teach her magic, and make choices that shape her personality and abilities.

Final choice: Seal her power, sacrifice yourself to contain it, or accept her power and fight the gods together.

E. The Villain Is the Player — Meta RPG + Mystery

Opening: The hero accepts quests, kills monsters, loots houses, and grinds for levels. Then NPCs begin questioning his actions: “Why did you kill three hundred slimes just to get stronger?”

Twist: The world is alive. Its inhabitants are conscious and realize the protagonist has been exploiting their lives like a game. The world itself becomes the final opponent.

Core question: What does being a hero mean when the people in the world do not experience it as a game?

03 · Recommended direction

THE GODBORN: A Nobody’s Destiny

Core promise: A nobody becomes powerful enough to challenge gods, but the thing he wants most is not a throne or a kingdom. He just wants his family back.
The opening hook

An ordinary orphan discovers that he possesses the power of an ancient god. He sets out to rescue his adoptive parents, only to discover that his family is connected to the secret behind the world’s destruction.

Story tone

Keep the main plot emotionally sincere. Let comedy come from character personalities, party arguments, strange side quests, monster abilities, and the contrast between the hero’s humble goals and the enormous stakes.

Central themes

  • Family is defined by love, not blood.
  • Power does not determine a person’s worth.
  • Being a hero means choosing whom to protect.
  • Even gods can misunderstand the meaning of humanity.
04 · Party members

Main characters

L

Leo — The Orphan

Main protagonist. Initially weak, stubborn, funny, and always hungry. Can absorb monster abilities. Wants to rescue the parents who raised him.

L

Luna — The Sister

A mysterious girl who travels with Leo. Looks innocent but may possess enough power to destroy entire armies. Her true identity is a major mystery.

K

Kai — The Rival

A royal knight who believes Leo is dangerous. Competitive and overly serious, he gradually becomes Leo’s closest friend.

P

Pip — The Merchant

A greedy little monster who joins the party because he thinks Leo will become rich. Surprisingly resourceful, but always looking for a deal.

06 · Signature gameplay

Monster Soul System

Leo absorbs abilities from monsters he defeats. Make the system useful in combat, exploration, and puzzles—then add a few ridiculous abilities for comic relief.

Fire Slime

Fireball — Launch a small explosive flame.

Ice Wolf

Ice Fang — Freeze or slow enemies.

Stone Golem

Iron Skin — Reduce incoming damage.

Shadow Demon

Shadow Step — Teleport a short distance.

Chicken Soul

Egg Production — Lay eggs. Surprisingly useful for cooking quests and one very strange puzzle.

Slime Soul

Bouncy Body — Bounce over gaps, but stairs become a serious problem.

Goblin Soul

Black-Market Charm — Get discounts from suspicious merchants.

Skeleton Soul

Poison Immunity — Immune to poison, but unable to eat food.

Design suggestion: Let players equip a limited number of souls at once. This creates build choices and makes each new monster ability feel meaningful instead of turning the protagonist into an unlimited collection of skills.

Other systems worth considering

  • Party banter: Short conversations during travel that reveal relationships and deliver humor.
  • Camp system: Cook, craft, talk to companions, and unlock personal scenes.
  • Monster research: Learn enemy weaknesses and discover unusual non-combat uses for abilities.
  • Choice-driven endings: Decisions affect trust, companion outcomes, and the final resolution.
  • Side quests: Mix heartfelt local stories with absurd requests and recurring comic characters.
