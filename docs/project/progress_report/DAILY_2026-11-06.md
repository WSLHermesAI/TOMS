## Watch out

- **The bow and the cells:** one finger cannot tap a cell while holding a pull. Arrow keys work on PC; a phone may feel awkward.
- **Tall phones:** the cells sit mid-screen, above the thumbs.
- **The three monsters** are not on any floor apart from floor 1's example, and their map and card icon is still the slime's. Their stats are copied, not tuned.
- **The floor 1 fight** may be hard for a new character. Delete `party` from that entry to undo it. Whether the stage editor keeps `party` when it saves is not checked.
- **The first-shot hint** ("往後拉再放開") overlaps the monster's name or HP bar until the first shot.
- **ComfyUI** was started for the painting and stopped afterwards. `--use` needs no server; `--paint` does.
