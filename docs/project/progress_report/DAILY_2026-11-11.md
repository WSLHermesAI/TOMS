## Watch out

- **Debug, first launch only:** the stage takes about 10 s while the sky's cache is made. A battle before that opens dark until the stage is ready.
- **Scripted runs keep the painted backdrop** (`--battle-3d=on` turns the stage on), so only the two new tests see the 3D stage. The old battle
  reference pictures are unchanged.
- **The web and Android** keep the painted backdrop (Q92). Not built or run there.
- **The 3D stage's files** (1.2 MB of Poly Haven CC0 art, 10 files) are in `assets/media/battle3d/`. The builder needs the network; its download cache is in `Build/art_cache`.
- **The sky as a JPG** clips values over 1: the glow at the horizon, behind the walls from this camera. A sky with a bright sun or moon in view would need the `.hdr` back.
- **Small props have 256 px textures:** fine up to their distance on this stage; one moved close to the camera would look soft.
- **During the danger close-up the hero is hidden** (the camera passes him), and Reduce effects turns the close-up off.
- **A click in the bands beside the canvas** cannot be fed by a test script, so it was checked by its logic and a band click scripted with a negative x.
- **The glTF renderer's change** (no draws for nodes scaled to nothing) was not checked against the glTF reference pictures: they are switched off.
- **The battle's 2D effects** follow the fighters on screen, not in the scene: a ruin cannot hide them yet (B3).
