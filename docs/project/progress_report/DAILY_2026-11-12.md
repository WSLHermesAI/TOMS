## Watch out

- **Inside the scene, an animation's layer effects** (glow, motion blur, outline) are drawn without the effect, and GPU particles are not
  drawn. Both work on screen pictures.
- **The fighters lie in one plane**, facing the camera: the light lights them as one surface, and they cast only the soft disc as shadow.
- **A monster's block can sit above its painted head** (the moss frog): its height in `battle.json` `"heights"` is taller than its picture.
- **The play scenarios that tap fixed points** (`battle_play_bow`, `battle_ultimate_free_*`, …) may need new points when they are switched back
  on: the plays moved to the top band.
- **The 2D battle** (3D off) also gets the top band and the head blocks; it was not looked at.
- **The web** was not checked after these changes.
