## Watch out

- **A symbol's times must rise** in its order; the editor warns when they do not.
- **Letters never rotate, reverse or mirror** (so they read as the letter); rings do.
- **The slide is judged on the circles' middles**: brushing a wrong circle's edge is fine, its middle is a miss.
