## Watch out

- **The web glTF viewer shares `Build\web-release-windows`** with the game. `build_web.cmd` takes a second argument,
  the target to build (`gltf_viewer`); without it, the game only.
- **On the web: no shadow maps, no MSAA.** Shadows were already off on WebGL (the 2026-10-05 link failure); MSAA would
  crash the page.
- **`fs_mesh.sc` sampler stages:** 7 and 8 the specular textures, 9 the environment cubemap (6 is the crowd's
  animation texture). A new texture needs a free stage.
- **The environment's orientation is the Khronos viewer's** (the picture's middle column looks down +Z); turning it in
  `gltf_environment.cpp` would make our screenshots differ from theirs.
- **With an environment on, the viewer's ambient slider and sky / ground colours do nothing**; its intensity is in the
  Environment section.
