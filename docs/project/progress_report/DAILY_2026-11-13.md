| 1 | **MP3 to OGG** ([22 §3](../../engine/22_AUDIO_GENERATION.md)) | `tools\convert_mp3_to_ogg.cmd` (`tools/audio/mp3_to_ogg.py`) converts every MP3 in a folder and all its sub-folders to an OGG beside it. Double-click it: a window picks the folder, then the frequency (keep, 44100, 32000, 22050, 16000 or 11025 Hz), music or sound effects, and whether to convert again. The last window gives the counts, the MB before and after, and every file whose OGG is not smaller, with both sizes. With a frequency, music is encoded by quality: at 160 kbps a lower rate was bigger, and 11025 Hz failed. A low-rate MP3 falls back to quality too. A frequency only goes down. A 20 s tone: 57 KB at 44100 Hz, 37 KB at 22050 Hz. `ogg.py` gained `convert()` and `sample_rate()` |

## Watch out

- **A special attack now takes 3.5 s longer** from the warning to the hit. The switched-off cell scenarios (`battle_cell_hit`, `battle_cell_dodge`, …)
  wait a fixed number of frames and will need longer runs when they are switched back on. The same goes for the play scenarios that tap fixed
  points (`battle_play_bow`, `battle_ultimate_free_*`): the prompts moved over the hero.
- **The knock-down's time is data, the clip is 1.6 s:** changing `downMs` does not change the clip.
- **The tool-made clips** (`power`, `charge`, `hurt_big`) are moved by bones and colour only. A painted pose of them would read better.
- **The MP3 converter keeps every OGG it writes**, even one bigger than its MP3; it only lists them.
