## Watch out

- **`ringY` depends on the hero's picture:** his picture is drawn taller than his box, so the ring's height is set from
  the box (-0.12). Another hero picture, or the portrait layout, may need another value; it was checked in landscape only.
- **One build failed and its test run passed on the old program** (a `//` comment hid a brace in `game_ui.cpp`). Fixed,
  rebuilt; the results above are from the new build.
- **The scenario's circles are drawn at fixed points** (the ring's middle at 140,359 at 1024×768): a change of the hero's
  box or `ringY` moves the ring, and `battle_ultimate_cyclone_full` must follow.
- **The camera pan and the "low" camera are 2D and 3D apart:** the pan (`value`) works on the 2D battle; on the 3D stage the
  existing "low" camera is used, and it was not checked there.
